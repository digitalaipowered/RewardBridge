Publisher Agreement

Effective July 26, 2026

Ownership and operator appointment

RewardBridge owns and controls its hosted survey portal, APIs, session infrastructure, provider integrations, fraud controls, ledgers, payout infrastructure, software, and documentation. The publisher retains ownership and responsibility for its separate application, website, domain, brand, content, and customer relationships. During the agreement term, the publisher appoints RewardBridge as technical operator of the hosted survey portal distributed through the approved application.

Publisher of record limitation

RewardBridge may act as publisher of record solely for its hosted survey portal only where the applicable survey provider expressly accepts that structure in writing. This agreement does not transfer ownership of the publisher’s application or create rights against a provider that has not approved the arrangement.

Approval and traffic sources

Every publisher and project is subject to review. Publishers must accurately disclose applications, domains, traffic sources, supported countries, user notices, reward rules, and integration methods. No project may send managed CPX traffic until provider and RewardBridge activation requirements are satisfied.

Economics

The initial allocation is 25% platform fee, 10% risk reserve, and 65% publisher pool from eligible gross provider revenue. The publisher chooses what portion of its publisher pool is assigned to end users, subject to platform limits.

Publisher obligations

  • Protect project credentials and report compromise.
  • Prevent duplicate accounts, automated traffic, answer manipulation, and deceptive earnings claims.
  • Display adequate privacy, reward, and provider notices.
  • Cooperate with provider reviews, fraud investigations, reversals, and support cases.

Suspension and termination

RewardBridge may suspend projects, sessions, rewards, or payouts for abnormal activity, provider instruction, security concerns, inaccurate disclosures, insufficient cleared provider funds, or agreement violations. Termination does not eliminate reserves, reversals, taxes, fraud investigations, payment returns, or unresolved liabilities.
