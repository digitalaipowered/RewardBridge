Publisher Agreement

CPX App 34813 is approved and active for RewardBridge. Each publisher, project, application origin, traffic source, and payout destination remains subject to RewardBridge review and operational controls.

Hosted portal and property

RewardBridge owns and controls the RewardBridge-hosted survey portal, APIs, provider integrations, financial ledgers, fraud systems, payout infrastructure, software, and documentation. The publisher retains ownership and responsibility for its separate website, app, domain, brand, content, customer relationships, and user accounts.

Operator appointment and license

During the agreement, the publisher appoints RewardBridge as the technical operator and administrator of the hosted survey portal distributed through the approved publisher property. The publisher grants a limited, revocable, nonexclusive license to use its approved name, branding, application URL, domain information, and integration surfaces solely to operate, secure, attribute, support, audit, and administer the service.

Provider condition

Where accepted by the applicable survey provider, RewardBridge may act as publisher of record solely for the hosted portal. This does not transfer ownership of the publisher’s app or domain and does not create rights against a provider that has not approved the arrangement. RewardBridge may require individual application or traffic-source review.

Clearing and publisher payment

Reported survey revenue is not publisher cash. RewardBridge recognizes publisher margin only from corresponding provider funds actually received, reconciled, and cleared, less valid reversals, payment costs, adjustments, taxes, prior payouts, and liabilities. When eligible cleared publisher balance reaches $25, the full eligible balance automatically enters one active payout review and is held from further use. Completion requires a verified destination and satisfactory review. Review timing may vary and does not guarantee immediate payment.

Destination and payment accuracy

The publisher must maintain an accurate payout destination under its control. A new or changed destination requires verification and may return an active payout to review. RewardBridge maintains review events, destination snapshots, payment references, and append-only ledger movements to reduce duplicate payment and destination-change risk.

Publisher duties

The publisher must accurately disclose its applications, traffic sources, reward representations, user notices, supported countries, and integration methods; protect API credentials; obtain required user consent; prevent misleading earnings claims; maintain legally adequate terms and privacy notices; and cooperate with provider reviews, fraud investigations, and verification.

Suspension and termination

RewardBridge may immediately suspend survey sessions, projects, credentials, balances, or payouts for suspected fraud, provider instructions, inaccurate information, excessive reversals, security risk, legal concerns, insufficient cleared funds, credential misuse, payment failure, or material breach. Termination does not eliminate valid reversals, reserves, disputes, audit obligations, or unpaid liabilities.

No general agency

Neither party may bind the other except for the limited hosted-portal operator appointment stated here. RewardBridge does not become owner of the publisher’s full site, app, domain, or unrelated operations.
