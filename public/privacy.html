Privacy Policy

Effective July 26, 2026

RewardBridge Network operates a hosted survey, attribution, ledger, fraud-control, and payout-administration platform for approved web publishers.

Information we process

  • Account, contact, identity, country, agreement, project, domain, and support information.
  • Opaque user identifiers, survey sessions, technical and fraud signals, provider transactions, rewards, reversals, and payout status.
  • Payment-destination information submitted for verification. Secret credentials are not stored in browser code.

CPX Research interaction

When surveys are activated, RewardBridge may transmit a stable opaque user identifier, IP address, user-agent information, country or profile information, and project attribution data to CPX Research for survey matching, quality control, fraud prevention, completion tracking, and postback communication. CPX Research may use cookies and collect response metadata, device information, survey answers, eligibility information, and technical logs under its own policies.

Review the CPX Research Privacy Policy and CPX Research User Terms.

Use and sharing

Information is used to authenticate accounts, operate projects, create sessions, attribute revenue, maintain financial records, investigate fraud, resolve support claims, and satisfy provider, payment, tax, security, and legal obligations. It may be shared with necessary service providers, professional advisers, and authorities. RewardBridge does not operate as an independent personal-data broker.

Retention and rights

Records are retained as needed for accounts, financial reconciliation, fraud defense, disputes, and legal compliance. Applicable rights may include access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection, subject to lawful retention requirements.
