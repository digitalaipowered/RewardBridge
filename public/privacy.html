Privacy Policy

RewardBridge Network is in controlled beta. Managed CPX traffic remains subject to platform activation controls. Publisher payout review begins automatically when eligible cleared publisher margin reaches $25.

Information we collect

We may collect account and profile information, publisher application details, application URLs, stable opaque user identifiers, project attribution, IP address, browser and device information, survey-session activity, transaction and reversal records, wallet and payout records, payout destinations, payment references, support communications, security events, and audit logs.

CPX Research interaction

When an approved survey session is active, RewardBridge may send CPX Research a stable opaque user identifier, project attribution values, the end user’s IP address and browser user-agent, and other information required to locate and secure eligible surveys. CPX may independently process survey responses, device information, cookies, identifiers, qualification data, and response metadata under its own policies.

Review the CPX Research Privacy Policy and CPX Research User Terms.

How information is used

We use information to authenticate accounts, operate hosted survey sessions, attribute transactions, calculate pending and cleared balances, prevent fraud, investigate reversals, begin payout review, verify payout destinations, confirm payment records, support publishers and end users, enforce agreements, maintain security, and comply with legal or provider requirements.

Payment processing

Payout destination and transaction information may be processed by PayPal or another approved payment provider. RewardBridge does not store a PayPal password. Payout records may include a destination snapshot, internal review history, status, and payment reference. Availability remains subject to identity, risk, tax, provider-funding, destination-verification, and payment requirements.

Sharing and sale

RewardBridge does not sell personal information for money. Information may be disclosed to survey providers, payment processors, infrastructure vendors, professional advisers, authorities when legally required, and parties involved in preventing fraud or protecting the platform. Each third party may process information under its own terms.

Retention and security

Financial ledgers, provider events, payout records, consent records, fraud evidence, and audit records may be retained as necessary for reconciliation, disputes, compliance, and security. We use access controls, row-level security, hashed credentials and identifiers, restricted secrets, append-only financial records, and logging, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Age and regional access

The initial RewardBridge service is intended for adults age 18 or older. Survey and payout availability may vary by country. We may block unsupported regions or require additional verification.

Your choices

You may request correction or deletion of eligible account data, subject to legal, financial, fraud-prevention, and recordkeeping obligations. Contact details appear on the Contact page.
