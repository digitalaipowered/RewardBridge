Rewards and Payout Terms

Survey reported → validation pending → provider payment pending → cash reconciled → available balance → automatic publisher review at $25 → processing → paid or action needed

Pending is not payable

A reported completion, browser message, postback, estimated amount, or dashboard display is not proof that RewardBridge received money. Pending rewards cannot be withdrawn and may be corrected, denied, or reversed.

Available rewards

A reward becomes available only after the corresponding provider transaction is validated and RewardBridge receives and reconciles the related provider funds. Additional security, identity, reserve, tax, destination, or payment checks may still apply.

Publisher review threshold

The RewardBridge publisher review threshold is $25 in eligible cleared publisher balance. At the threshold, the full eligible balance enters review automatically; a publisher does not submit a separate withdrawal request. The reviewed amount moves from available to held and cannot be included in another payout review.

Publisher payout status

Publisher-facing statuses are simplified as Under review, Processing, Paid, or Action needed. Under review includes destination and risk checks. Processing means the payout has been approved and payment completion is being confirmed. Paid means a payment reference and settlement record were stored. Action needed means information, destination verification, or issue resolution is required.

Reversals and negative adjustments

Provider reversals, duplicate transactions, fraud findings, payment failures, disputes, or calculation corrections may reduce pending or available balances and may be recovered from reserves, future earnings, or unpaid payout reviews. RewardBridge retains transaction history and records adjustments through compensating ledger entries.

Failed or rejected reviews

When payment cannot be completed or a review is rejected, held funds may return to available balance through compensating entries. RewardBridge may pause further payout review until the destination, risk, legal, or payment issue is resolved.

End-user payout availability

Managed end-user automatic payouts are not active during the initial controlled beta. Publishers must not promise instant or guaranteed payment. When an end-user payout method is introduced, it may require identity verification, a supported country, a verified destination, a minimum balance, and risk review.

Taxes, fees, and timing

Recipients are responsible for taxes and required reporting. Disclosed payment-processing charges or legally required withholding may apply. Passing a threshold or entering review does not guarantee a specific payment date.
