Rewards Terms

Effective July 26, 2026

Reward states

Provider-reported activity is pending and is not cash. Amounts may move through reported, validated, provider-payment-pending, cash-received, reserve, available, requested, paid, reversed, rejected, or failed states.

Availability

A reward becomes available only after the applicable survey revenue is received and reconciled, required holds are satisfied, and fraud or reversal checks are complete. Browser messages and provider postbacks do not independently create withdrawable funds.

Minimums

The minimum publisher withdrawal is $25. Each publisher may set its own end-user payout minimum at $2 or higher.

Reversals and adjustments

Invalid responses, duplicate participation, provider reversals, payment returns, fraud, clerical corrections, or reconciliation differences may reduce pending or future balances. RewardBridge uses compensating ledger entries rather than deleting transaction history.

Payouts and disputes

Payout destinations must be verified. Payouts may be delayed or rejected because of identity, tax, sanctions, country, fraud, provider, funding, or payment-processor requirements. Missing-reward reports should include the account, approximate completion time, survey reference, and relevant screenshots, but never passwords or API secrets.

No earnings guarantee

Survey availability and qualification vary. RewardBridge and participating publishers must not promise fixed earnings, continuous surveys, or guaranteed payout dates.
