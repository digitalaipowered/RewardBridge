Platform Terms

Effective July 26, 2026

RewardBridge is in controlled beta. Managed CPX traffic is disabled until written provider approval and operational configuration are complete.

Eligibility and accounts

Users and publishers must provide accurate information, control their access email, comply with law, and complete any required identity, tax, payment, domain, or application verification.

Permitted use

RewardBridge may be used only for approved applications, legitimate users, authorized survey distribution, and truthful reward programs. Duplicate or automated participation, false traffic, answer manipulation, geographic evasion, misleading earnings claims, and interference with fraud controls are prohibited.

Provider dependency

Survey availability, qualification, validation, reversals, pricing, and payment schedules are controlled by third-party providers. Provider-reported activity remains pending until the corresponding cash is received and reconciled.

Balances and payouts

Publisher withdrawals require at least $25 in cleared available publisher balance. End-user minimums are selected by each publisher but cannot be below $2. Pending, held, reserve, disputed, reversed, or unreceived amounts are not withdrawable.

Suspension and liability

RewardBridge may pause accounts, projects, sessions, rewards, or payouts for fraud, security risk, inaccurate information, provider instruction, legal risk, insufficient cleared funds, or material breach. The service is provided on an as-available basis, subject to rights that cannot legally be excluded.
