# Tests
## Test 1 - Public economics load [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Description: Verifies the active CPX landing page exposes the automatic publisher review threshold and fixed reward model.
Steps:
1. Navigate to the app
2. Wait for the landing page to load
3. Review the economics section
Expected: The page shows automatic publisher payout review at $25, a $2 end-user floor, a 45% completion reward, and a 100% user bonus pass-through.
## Test 2 - Personal email access and legal links are visible
Viewport: mobile
Description: Confirms publishers can access the privacy page, personal-email sign-in, and the active CPX rollout notice on mobile.
Steps:
1. Navigate to the app on mobile
2. Scroll to Publisher access
3. Open Privacy and verify the CPX disclosure appears
Expected: The page accepts personal email addresses, Privacy opens with CPX processing disclosure, and the site states CPX App 34813 is approved and active.
## Test 3 - Invalid survey session is rejected
Viewport: desktop
Description: Verifies an invalid hosted survey session fails closed without exposing survey links or an embedded provider wall.
Steps:
1. Navigate to the app with ?session=invalid-session
2. Wait for the session exchange to finish
3. Review the portal result
Expected: The portal shows Survey portal unavailable and does not render survey cards, survey links, or an iframe.
